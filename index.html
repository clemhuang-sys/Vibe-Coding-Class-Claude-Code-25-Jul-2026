Questions

Before you ask

Is the growth diagnostic really free?

Yes. No fee, no credit card, no obligation. You get a one-page read on where your growth is leaking and the first move we'd make.

What happens after I submit the form?

A consultant — not a sales rep — reads your note and replies within one working day with your diagnostic. If it's useful, we can talk. If not, the diagnostic is yours to keep.

Who is Meridian & Co. for?

B2B and considered-purchase consumer brands, usually between $1M and $50M in revenue, with a product that works and growth that has stalled.

Will you try to sell me something?

We'll tell you the three things holding your pipeline back whether or not you hire us. The diagnostic stands on its own.

Is this a real company?

No. Meridian & Co. is a fictional brand built to demonstrate a website design. The enquiry form runs in demo mode and doesn't send or store anything, so please don't enter real personal details.
